# Why the coaching works the way it does

The research behind the page's order, tone and the one-focus rule. Metric guides and their sources are in
numbers.md; this file is about how feedback changes behaviour in senior people who aren't trained salespeople.

- **Feedback can make performance worse.** Over a third of feedback interventions lowered
  performance; effects drop the more feedback points at the person instead of the task (Kluger &
  DeNisi 1996, 607 effects). → critique the moment and the behaviour, never the person.
  https://cris.huji.ac.il/en/publications/the-effects-of-feedback-interventions-on-performance-a-historical/
- **Task and "how to do it better" beat praise.** Feedback about the self is the least effective;
  feed-forward ("where to next") the most (Hattie & Timperley 2007). → every issue ends with the exact line to say next time.
  https://conselhopedagogico.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/files/sites/32/hattie-and-timperley-2007.pdf
- **Future-focused talks motivate; arguing the past entrenches.** People given negative feedback
  doubted its accuracy and disagreed more about the past afterwards; motivation to change tracked how
  much the talk was about future actions (Gnepp et al. 2020). https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0234444
- **A real strength first lowers defensiveness — only if it comes before the criticism** (Critcher,
  Dunning & Armor 2010). https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/sasi/wp-content/uploads/sites/275/2015/11/Critcher_afftiming.pdf
- **One or two behaviours at a time.** Coaching many at once changes nothing (Gong coaching data);
  overloaded weak performers stalled with an AI coach and improved when feedback was restricted (Luo
  et al. 2021, 429 agents); people improve only on items they set goals for (Smither et al. 2005).
  https://www.gong.io/blog/data-driven-call-coaching-habits-of-effective-sales-leaders ·
  https://www.eurasiareview.com/21112020-coaching-sales-agents-use-ai-and-human-coaches/ ·
  https://leeds-faculty.colorado.edu/dahe7472/smither%20performance.pdf
- **Bad coaching does twice the damage good coaching helps** (CEB/Challenger). Accuracy and evidence
  matter more than volume. https://challengerinc.com/coaching-in-sales/
- **Repetition with feedback on a defined sub-skill** is what builds skill; experience alone doesn't
  (Ericsson 1993, 2008). → a focus behaviour, a measurable "fixed" line, a drill.
- **AI plus a human beats either alone**, and the most senior people resist AI feedback most (Luo
  2021; Tong et al. 2021). One visible AI error destroys trust faster than a human's (Dietvorst 2015).
  → every claim quote-verified; a 15-minute weekly human discussion of the focus.
- **Execs and experts:** status threat (SCARF), curse of knowledge and feature dumping (Bain Capital
  Ventures; Kazanjy, *Founding Sales* ch. 7), founders "talking at the prospect for an hour" (First
  Round). → turn expertise into questions; keep demos and explanations short.
  https://baincapitalventures.com/insight/mind-the-gap-the-curse-of-knowledge-and-the-ai-sales-challenge/ ·
  https://www.foundingsales.com/7-pitching ·
  https://review.firstround.com/the-most-common-go-to-market-questions-from-founders/
